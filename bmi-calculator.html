Understanding your result

What your BMI result means

For adults, BMI is grouped into ranges that can be used as a screening measure. A result is only one piece of information and does not account for every difference in body composition or health.

If you have questions about what your result means for you, a healthcare professional can help put it in context.

Limitations of BMI

Although BMI can be a practical screening measure, it does not directly measure body fat or describe a person’s overall health. It may not reflect differences in muscle mass, age, or individual circumstances.

Adult BMI ranges are not intended for children and teens. If you are pregnant, or unsure how to interpret a result, ask a healthcare professional for personal guidance.

♀

Age & growth

Children and teens need age- and sex-specific BMI charts rather than adult categories.

◉

Muscle

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so it can be less informative for some people.

♡

Pregnancy

This adult calculator is not designed to assess BMI during pregnancy.

✳

Individual context

Health history and other factors matter when interpreting a screening result.
